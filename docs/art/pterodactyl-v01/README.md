# The T-Rex Game — Pterodactyl V01

Small orange flying prey character, deliberately **32×32** versus T-Rex **64×64**. This is a real alpha-transparent sprite pack, with 21 frames and a 192×160 atlas. It is a simplified playable pixel-art interpretation of the approved concept, **not** an extraction of the concept image.

## Animations
- fly: 6 frames @ 10 FPS, looping
- glide: 4 frames @ 5 FPS, looping
- dive: 4 frames @ 12 FPS, one-shot
- hurt: 3 frames @ 8 FPS, one-shot
- eaten: 4 frames @ 12 FPS, one-shot (final frame transparent)

Frame cells: 32×32 pixels, facing right. Atlas has six columns and five rows. Animation rectangles and frame rates are in `assets/characters/pterodactyl/pterodactyl_atlas.json`.

Transparent pixels are real PNG alpha, not a checkerboard. Preview is enlarged for inspection only.
